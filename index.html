What is Bridge Mantle?

Mantle Bridge is the official Mantle Network app for moving supported assets between Ethereum Mainnet and Mantle, documented in the official bridge guide. The production action happens on app.mantle.xyz/bridge.

How Bridge Mantle works

  1. Open the appUse the official app URL and confirm the domain is app.mantle.xyz before connecting a wallet.
  2. Connect walletConnect your Web3 wallet, then choose the Ethereum-to-Mantle direction or the reverse withdrawal flow.
  3. Select assetPick a supported token, enter the amount, and review any approval request before signing.
  4. Confirm and trackSubmit the wallet transaction, monitor status, and complete any required prove or claim step for withdrawals.

Built for Ethereum users

Bridge Mantle FAQ

What is bridge mantle?

bridge mantle is search intent for using Mantle Bridge, the official app route for moving supported assets between Ethereum and Mantle Network.

Is bridge mantle the official Mantle app?

The official destination is app.mantle.xyz/bridge; verify that domain before connecting a wallet or approving tokens.

How do I bridge mantle assets from Ethereum?

Open the official app, connect your wallet, choose Ethereum as the source and Mantle as the destination, select a supported asset, then confirm in your wallet.

Can I withdraw with bridge mantle?

Yes. The bridge supports the reverse flow from Mantle back to Ethereum, and withdrawals can include additional prove and claim steps before funds arrive on L1.

What should I check before using bridge mantle?

Confirm the app domain, review token approvals, keep enough gas for the source network, and start with a small transfer if you are testing the flow.
