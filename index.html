Mantle Bridge.

The Mantle Bridge brings ETH, USDC and tokens onto Mantle, a modular layer-2 that posts transaction data to its own data-availability layer instead of all to Ethereum, which is how it keeps fees low. You see the amount received and the fee before signing, and keep your own keys throughout.

How the Mantle Bridge fits Mantle's modular design

Unlike a monolithic rollup, Mantle splits the job: execution on the L2 and transaction data on a separate data-availability layer. The Mantle Bridge deposits your asset on Ethereum and credits it on Mantle, where that modular design is the reason gas stays low even when activity is high.

MNT: the gas token you need after the Mantle Bridge

Mantle uses MNT, not ETH, to pay for gas on the network. After you bridge in, keep a little MNT so you can transact; many users bridge a small amount alongside their main asset, or acquire MNT on a Mantle DEX once they arrive.

Deposit and withdrawal flow on the Mantle Bridge

Deposits credit on Mantle within minutes of your Ethereum transaction confirming. Standard withdrawals back to Ethereum follow the rollup's finalization before funds release, so the exit side is slower than the entry. The current estimate is shown before you commit.

What a Mantle Bridge transfer costs

You pay Ethereum gas to deposit; on Mantle itself fees are low thanks to the modular data-availability approach, paid in MNT. Compare routes by the net amount received after Ethereum gas and any protocol fee rather than the headline figure.

The Mantle ecosystem after you bridge

On Mantle your assets work across its DeFi — DEXs, lending, liquidity and MNT-denominated apps. Hold a little MNT for gas, and when you want native ETH on mainnet again, exit through the canonical withdrawal route.
What makes Mantle modular?
Mantle separates execution from data availability, posting transaction data to its own DA layer, which helps keep fees low.
Do I need MNT after the Mantle Bridge?
Yes. Gas on Mantle is paid in MNT, so keep a small amount to transact once your assets arrive.
Why is a Mantle withdrawal slower than a deposit?
Deposits credit after Ethereum confirmations; withdrawals wait for the rollup to finalize before releasing funds to Ethereum.
Do I need an account to use the Mantle Bridge?
No. You connect a self-custody wallet and bridge on-chain, with no sign-up and no custody.
What if my deposit fails?
It reverts and you keep your assets; only the Ethereum gas for the attempt is spent.
